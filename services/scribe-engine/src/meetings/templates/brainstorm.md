## Summary

Every distinct idea raised, one bullet each, named after who pitched it ("Name: idea"). Keep half-formed and joking ideas too; mark the ones the group got excited about with "(strong)". End with one bullet naming the single idea with the most momentum and why.

## Decisions

Ideas the group chose to pursue or explicitly killed, each with `[MM:SS]` and the one-line reason.

## Action items

Experiments, research or prototypes someone agreed to try, with `{owner}` and `[MM:SS]`.

## Open questions

Unknowns that would change which idea wins: what to test, measure or ask someone before committing.

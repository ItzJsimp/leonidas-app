## Summary

What each person is working on, how it is going, and anything blocking them. One bullet per person, then one bullet on the mood or energy of the call if it came up.

## Decisions

Agreements about priorities, ownership or next steps, each citing its `[MM:SS]` timestamp.

## Action items

Every commitment either person made ("I'll", "let me", "can you"), with `{owner}` and `[MM:SS]`. Include favors and small promises; those are the ones that get forgotten.

## Open questions

Anything one person asked that the other could not answer yet, and topics flagged for the next check-in.

## Summary

Who the other side is and what they want; their budget, timeline and decision process if mentioned; objections or hesitations they raised; and the exact numbers quoted by either side (price, rates, deliverables, dates). Quote the other side's own words where it matters.

## Decisions

Terms agreed, offers made or accepted, and anything ruled out, each citing its `[MM:SS]` timestamp.

## Action items

Follow-ups promised by either side (send a proposal, share a rate card, intro someone), with `{owner}` and `[MM:SS]`. List the other side's commitments too.

## Open questions

What still blocks a yes: unanswered questions, missing info, and who has to sign off.

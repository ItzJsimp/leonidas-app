# Record and read your meetings in Leonidas

Leonidas now has a Meetings tab. It records your calls, writes down who said what, and keeps clear notes you can read later.

## Record a call

Open **Meetings** in the sidebar and press **Record meeting**. Leonidas records your microphone and the sound of the call, and the text shows up as people talk. A red "Recording meeting" line stays at the top until you press **Stop**.

When Discord or another call app starts using your microphone, Leonidas asks if you want to record the call. It never starts recording on its own. Recording other people can need their consent, so tell them first.

## Read what was said

When you stop, Leonidas writes notes with a summary, decisions, action items and open questions. The transcript below the notes shows each speaker: you, and the other people as separate voices.

Your meetings and their audio stay on your computer. Audio is deleted after 30 days unless you press **Keep audio**. The notes and transcript stay.

## Your old meetings come with you

Meetings you recorded with the old Scribe app show in the same list, with their notes and transcripts.
